
# AI Models Documentation

## Overview

The Automated Single-Image to 3D Asset and Point Cloud Generation System integrates five AI models. Each model performs one specific task and passes its output to the next stage of the pipeline.

The processing sequence is:

Input Image
↓

Florence-2

↓

GroundingDINO

↓

Florence-2 (Part Detection)

↓

SAM2.1

↓

rembg

↓

Hunyuan3D-2

↓

GLB Mesh

↓

Open3D

↓

DBSCAN

---

# Model 1 — Florence-2

## Purpose

Florence-2 performs two independent tasks.

1. Automatic Image Captioning
2. Object Part Detection

---

## Why Florence-2?

Advantages

- Open vocabulary
- High accuracy
- No manual prompts
- Multiple vision tasks
- Lightweight compared to many VLMs
