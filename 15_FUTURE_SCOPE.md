
# Future Scope

## Overview

Future versions can extend the platform into a complete AI-powered 3D content generation ecosystem.

# Vision

The long-term vision is to evolve the project from a research prototype into a production-ready AI platform capable of generating high-quality 3D assets from various input modalities (single image, multiple images, and video) for applications in AR/VR, robotics, digital twins, industrial inspection, education, and e-commerce.
